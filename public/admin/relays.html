Relays

There are two relays, each combining Under-12, Under-14 and Under-17: one for boys and one for girls. Pick the relay, make each team from any four of its swimmers, then enter the team's time. Teams are ranked by time on the public Results page, and every swimmer in a 1st, 2nd or 3rd team gets a merit certificate.
